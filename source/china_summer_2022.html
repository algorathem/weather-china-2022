What is in the file

This page is the spreadsheet, drawn. Double-click the file to open it. It does not call a server, and the weather never leaves the computer.

Four names in the spreadsheet belong to two towns each: Ji'an in Jilin and Jiangxi, Jinghe in Xinjiang and Shaanxi, Yichun in Heilongjiang and Jiangxi, and two places both called Bugt in Nei Mongol. They are separated here by their coordinates.

All 791 missing temperatures belong to seven Taiwan stations. Their rain is on the map in sand, and in the rain ranking. Every other station has a full temperature record.

Rainfall in the spreadsheet arrived with tiny binary fractions, so figures here are rounded to 0.1 mm. Averages skip blank days. Units are degrees Celsius and millimetres.
